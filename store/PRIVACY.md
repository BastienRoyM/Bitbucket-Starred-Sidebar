# Privacy policy: Starred Sidebar for Bitbucket

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Starred Sidebar for Bitbucket is a browser extension that adds a list of your starred Bitbucket repositories to Bitbucket's sidebar.

## What it accesses

When you open a page on bitbucket.org, the extension uses your existing Bitbucket session to read:

- your list of starred repositories (names, links and avatars),
- your Bitbucket account ID, only to count pull requests you wrote or are reviewing,
- the number of open pull requests you wrote or are reviewing in those repositories.

## Where it goes

Nowhere. All requests go only to bitbucket.org. The extension has no server and does not send data to the developer or to any third party. It does not use analytics or tracking.

## What it stores

In your browser's extension storage:

- your settings (which links to show, whether the section is pinned, whether Bitbucket's sidebar is collapsed),
- the last loaded list of starred repositories and pull request counts, so the sidebar shows instantly.

Your settings may sync between your own Chrome browsers through your Google account. Uninstalling the extension deletes the stored data.
